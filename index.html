Braces for Kids

Fun, colorful WildSmiles braces that kids actually look forward to. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first check-up no later than age 7.

  • Shapes and colors kids can pick
  • Gentle guidance while jaws are still growing
  • Parents welcome in every visit

Braces for Adults & Seniors

It is never too late for a straighter smile. Choose clear or traditional braces, and enjoy the everyday benefits:

  • Easier cleaning
  • Lower risk of decay and gum disease
  • More comfort when chewing and speaking
  • Less uneven tooth wear
  • Possible relief from TMJ-related headaches
Request an appointment (opens in a new tab)

Clear Aligners

Spark aligners: we hide your treatment, not your smile. Wear them 20 to 22 hours a day, and treatment often takes 24 months or less.

  • Nearly invisible and removable
  • Smooth, comfortable material

Not ideal for small teeth, severe misalignment, TMJ problems or implants. We will tell you honestly if they fit your case.

Find out if aligners fit (opens in a new tab)

Bite & Jaw Correction

A healthy bite helps you chew, speak and smile with ease. We treat open bite, crossbite, overbite and underbite using palatal expanders, Herbst appliances, braces and aligners.

  • Early treatment can guide growth
  • Most cases never need surgery

Surgery is the last resort, only when nothing else works, and always through referral to a trusted oral surgeon.

Book a bite evaluation (opens in a new tab)

Retainers

Your new smile needs a loyal guard. We offer three types, so there is a fit for every lifestyle:

  • Lingual: hidden behind the teeth
  • Clear: nearly invisible
  • Hawley: the classic, wire and acrylic
Ask about retainers (opens in a new tab)

Technology

Smarter braces, better smiles

Pitts21: the sliding door

Old-style braces hold the wire with tiny elastic ties that create friction. Pitts21 uses a frictionless sliding door, so teeth glide into place.

  • Less discomfort
  • Easier cleaning
  • Fewer extractions
  • Faster treatment

Smile Arc Protection

A youthful smile has a wide, natural curve. Traditional treatment can leave it narrow, showing only 6 teeth. Smile Arc Protection widens the curve so 12 teeth show, and teeth set at the right angle also make a better bite.

Kids Corner

A first visit is not always braces

Around age 7, baby and permanent teeth share the mouth. That is the perfect moment for an orthodontist to peek at how things are growing, and to step in early only if it helps.

Growing up, one smile at a time
